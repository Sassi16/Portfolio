About

Turning data into decisions that make sense.

Currently a B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Dr. Mahalingam College of Engineering and Technology, Pollachi, I spend most of my time somewhere between a dataset and a codebase — pulling structure out of big data, visualizing it, training models on it, then building the web interfaces that put it in front of people.

What pulled me toward explainable AI specifically is a simple bias: a prediction without a reason isn't very useful to the person who has to act on it. That's the thread running through my projects. When I'm not deep in coursework, I'm usually testing that idea on a new dataset, chipping away at a campus-facing side project, or on a volleyball court.
